# S4 Sprint Report: Scarcity, Deploy & Observability
Date: 2026-11-21
Platform: Fuel Supply Intelligence & Resilience Platform (FSIP)

## Features Delivered
- **F-009: Finite Supply & Rationing Policy (Finish)**
  - `GET /api/cover` endpoint returning network and fuel days of cover, supply-ends tick (212), and rationing status.
  - Dedicated "Cover & Scarcity" UI panel with timeline progress towards Tick 212 and single-route isolation alerts.
  - Dynamic heuristic switch to minimum service level protection mode during scarcity.
- **F-011: k6 Load Test Suite**
  - Production load test script under `deploy/k6/load.js` and `loadtest/k6.js`.
  - Benchmark profiles recorded under `docs/results/`.
- **F-012: Deploy to Vercel & Render**
  - Render blueprint (`deploy/render.yaml`) covering Postgres 18, simulator, API, and worker.
  - Vercel SPA routing configuration (`deploy/vercel.json`).
- **F-013: Operator Identity & Hardening**
  - Multi-operator token support (`OPERATOR_TOKENS=name:token,...`) with actor audit tracking.
  - Startup refusal for default placeholder tokens in non-local environments.
  - Chaos endpoints strictly gated behind `DEV_TOOLS_ENABLED`.
- **F-017: Grafana Dashboards & Prometheus Alerts**
  - Provisioned dashboards for golden signals, dispatch outbox, and forecast accuracy.
  - Pre-configured alerting rules for stockout risk, data staleness, and circuit breaker trip.
